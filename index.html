The name is the method

Arabesque patterns look intricate, but they come from a few simple shapes and one rule, repeated with care. No line is there for decoration. Each one is where the geometry puts it.

We build software the same way: small, well-made modules, clear interfaces between them, and a system that stays coherent as it grows.

  1. 1 Tile the plane with simple shapes.

    In software: modules.

  2. 2 From each edge's midpoint, draw two lines at a fixed angle.

    In software: interfaces.

  3. 3 Remove the scaffolding. The pattern holds.

    In software: the system.

How we build

Four stages, repeated as many times as it takes to get something right.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Start with the real problem, not the assumed solution. We take time to understand the constraints before writing a line of code.

  2. 2

    Design

    Structure comes before pixels. We sketch the system's shape, its data, flows and edge cases, so the build has a foundation.

  3. 3

    Build

    Small, deliberate increments instead of big-bang releases. Working software, reviewed and tested at every step.

  4. 4

    Refine

    The first version is rarely the right one. We revisit, simplify and polish until the details hold up.
