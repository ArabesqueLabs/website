About Us

An independent software studio

Arabesque Labs is a small, independent studio building a new piece of software from the ground up. We're early — deliberately so. Right now, most of our energy is going into the product itself rather than talking about it.

The name comes from the geometric patterns found in Islamic art: intricate structures built from simple, repeating rules. That's the kind of software we want to make — systems that feel coherent, considered, and built to last, rather than assembled in a hurry.

We're not chasing every trend or shipping for the sake of shipping. We'd rather take the time to build something we're proud to put our name on.
